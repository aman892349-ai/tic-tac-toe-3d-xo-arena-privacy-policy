Privacy Policy

Tic Tac Toe 3D: XO Arena

Effective date: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tic Tac Toe 3D: XO Arena (the “App”) handles information when you use the game.

1. Information We Collect

The App is designed for offline gameplay. In its current version, it does not ask you to create an account and does not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, phone number, email address, precise location, or contacts.

2. Game Data and Storage

Gameplay information, such as scores or game settings, may be kept locally on your device if those features are enabled in the version you use. This information is not sent to our servers by the App. Removing the App or clearing its data may delete locally stored information.

3. Internet, Analytics, and Advertising

The App is intended to work offline and currently is not designed to use advertising SDKs, analytics services, or a remote backend. If a future version adds advertising, analytics, online features, or other third-party services, this policy will be updated before those features are introduced, as applicable.

4. Third-Party Services

The App does not intentionally share your personal information with third parties. Your device's operating system or app distribution platform may process information independently under its own privacy terms.

5. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users. If you believe personal information has been provided to us in error, contact us using the details below so we can review the concern.

6. Data Security

Because the App is designed for offline use and does not operate its own account or game-data server, game data intended to remain on your device is subject to the security of your device and its operating system. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's features or legal requirements change. The updated policy will be published at this page with a revised effective date.
